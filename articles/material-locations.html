Subnautica 2 Material Locations: Where to Find Everything

Knowing where to find materials saves hours of aimless swimming. This guide maps every early-game resource to its biome and depth range.

Titanium

Best Biome: Grassy Plateau (10-100m)

Rarity: Common

Titanium is the most abundant resource in Subnautica 2. It spawns everywhere in the shallow biomes but is most concentrated in the Grassy Plateau. Look for metallic-gray deposits on the seafloor. You can also salvage Titanium from wreckage and debris scattered around your starting area.

Collection tip: Titanium deposits yield 2 units each. A full stack (10 units) supports most early-game crafting. Collect it whenever you see it.

Copper

Best Biome: Cave entrances in Grassy Plateau (30-200m)

Rarity: Uncommon

Copper appears as reddish-brown nodules near cave entrances and along rocky outcrops. It's less common than Titanium but essential for the Scanner and Battery. Check the edges of cave systems in the Grassy Plateau and Sparse Reef.

Collection tip: Copper deposits are smaller and harder to spot. Use your Scanner to locate them from a distance. They reflect light differently than surrounding rock.

Quartz

Best Biome: Sandstone caves (20-150m)

Rarity: Common

Quartz appears as clear, crystalline formations in sandstone cave walls. It's used to craft Glass, which is needed for windows, water filtration, and Oxygen Tanks.

Collection tip: Quartz deposits are bright and easy to see. They're most abundant in cave systems with sandstone walls and ceilings.

Rubber

Best Biome: Kelp Forest (50-200m)

Rarity: Uncommon

Rubber is found as dark, fibrous organic deposits attached to kelp stalks and rock formations. It's essential for crafting Fins and other mobility equipment.

Collection tip: The Kelp Forest is rich in Rubber but patrolled by predators. Collect quickly and stay aware of your surroundings. Bring extra oxygen — the Kelp Forest can be disorienting.

Silver

Best Biome: Deep caves (100-300m)

Rarity: Rare

Silver is needed for wiring and advanced electronics. It spawns in deeper cave systems. You'll encounter it naturally as you explore deeper biomes.

Gold

Best Biome: Thermal vents (200-400m)

Rarity: Very Rare

Gold is found near thermal vent activity. These are dangerous areas with high temperatures and aggressive creatures. Save gold collection for when you have better equipment.

💡 Quick Reference

Depth order for material hunting: Titanium (10m) → Rubber (50m) → Copper (30m) → Quartz (20m) → Silver (100m) → Gold (200m). Follow this order and you'll always be gathering materials you can use immediately.
