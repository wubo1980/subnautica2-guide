Subnautica 2 Beginner's Guide — How to Craft Survival Multitool + Scanner + Fins

Complete beginner guide: what to build first, materials & order. After reading: Multitool + Scanner + Fins equipped, Fabricator located, Titanium stocked.

Understanding Hunger and Thirst in Subnautica 2

Hunger and thirst are the second survival system you need to master after oxygen. Unlike oxygen, which depletes constantly, hunger and thirst operate on a slower timer — but they're equally lethal if ignored. This guide explains exactly how the system works, why eating doesn't always seem to help, and how to keep yourself fed and hydrated.

How the Hunger and Thirst System Works

Your character has two stats: Food (hunger) and Water (thirst). Both are displayed as meters in your inventory screen. They deplete over time, with water typically draining faster than food. A full Food meter lasts approximately 10-15 minutes of gameplay, while a full Water meter lasts about 8-12 minutes.

Why Eating Doesn't Instantly Restore the Meter

Many new players notice that eating a fish doesn't immediately fill the hunger meter to full. This is not a bug — it's intentional game design. The meters represent a survival timer, not an instant resource. Eating or drinking provides gradual restoration over approximately 5-10 seconds, not an instant refill. This means eating one fish won't fill your hunger bar completely — you need multiple food sources, and the effect accumulates over time.

Meter Levels and Effects

50-100%: Normal. No penalties. Health regenerates normally.
25-50%: Minor penalties. Slight energy reduction, slower health regeneration. Your character moves at normal speed but stamina recovers slower.
10-25%: Moderate penalties. Energy drops noticeably. Health stops regenerating entirely. Your vision begins to blur slightly.
0-10%: Critical. Health begins to drain. Vision darkens significantly. Movement speed slows. You have approximately 60 seconds to eat or drink before death.
0%: Health drains rapidly. Death occurs within ~60 seconds if not resolved.

Food Sources: Complete Guide

Peepers — Best Early Food Source

Peepers are small, fast-swimming fish found in the Grassy Plateau and Safe Shallows. They're your primary food source for the first several hours. They're abundant, easy to catch by hand, and spawn frequently. Food value: Raw = ~15 food. Cooked (1 Peeper + 1 Salt at Fabricator) = ~30 food. Always cook your fish. Cooked food restores double the value of raw food.

Saltade Plants

Saltade plants grow in the Kelp Forest at 20-50m depth. They're stationary and easy to harvest. Food value: ~20 food per harvest. They respawn after a short time, making them a reliable renewable food source for players based near the Kelp Forest.

Bladderfish — Primary Water Source

Small balloon-like fish in the Safe Shallows. Process at the Fabricator (Survival tab) for ~15 water units. Bladderfish are common, easy to catch, and respawn quickly. They're your most reliable early water source. Tip: Carry 2-3 Bladderfish when leaving on any expedition.

Cured Fish

Recipe: 2x Salt + 1x Raw Fish. Food value: ~35 food. Cured fish lasts significantly longer before spoiling and provides the highest food-per-slot ratio of any early-game food source. It's ideal for long expeditions away from your Lifepod.

Nutrient Block

Recipe: 2x Creepvine Seed Cluster + 1x Titanium. Food value: ~50 food. Nutrient Blocks are high-density food bars designed for extended exploration. They don't spoil and provide massive food value. Creepvine Seed Clusters are found in the Kelp Forest — they glow yellow on the vine stalks near the surface.

Water Sources: Complete Guide

Water Bottle — Your Reusable Solution

Recipe: 2x Titanium + 1x Salt. Water value: ~25 units per fill. The Water Bottle is a reusable container. Once crafted, you can refill it at specific freshwater sources marked on your map. It's far more efficient than relying solely on Bladderfish. When to craft: As soon as you have Titanium to spare, craft this and carry it everywhere.

Bladderfish Water

Recipe: 1x Bladderfish. Water value: ~15 units. Your go-to water source before you craft the Water Bottle. Always carry a few extra Bladderfish when exploring.

Disinfected Water

Recipe: 1x Bladderfish + 1x Salt. Water value: ~20 units. A purified version that provides more water per Bladderfish. Requires a Water Filtration Machine or Fabricator modification. Craft these for longer expeditions.

Angel Comb: What It Is and How to Unlock It

The Angel Comb is a rare item that grants a permanent health regeneration buff and unlocks several advanced crafting recipes. Finding it requires preparation but the reward is substantial.

What the Angel Comb Does

  • Grants a permanent health regeneration buff (slow, continuous HP restoration — roughly 1 HP every 5 seconds)
  • Unlocks several advanced crafting recipes that require its unique properties
  • Serves as a key item for end-game story progression

How to Find It

Location: A coral shrine in a deep cave biome, approximately 200-400m depth. The shrine is a small, ornate structure containing the Comb on a pedestal.

Required equipment: High-Capacity O₂ Tank (recommended — the depth makes oxygen management critical), Scanner (to identify the shrine), Survival Multitool (to clear obstacles), and at least 3 Beacons (to mark your path).

Navigation: Enter the cave system through a large opening at the edge of the Grassy Plateau where it meets the deeper biome. The coral shrine is at the bottom of a long vertical shaft. Drop Beacons at every major junction to ensure you can find your way back.

Hazards: The cave contains aggressive creatures that patrol the shaft. Do not engage them — use your Fins to swim past and focus on reaching the shrine. The water pressure at this depth significantly increases oxygen depletion, so plan your route carefully.

⚠️ Angel Comb Expedition Warning

The coral shrine cave is deep (200-400m) and requires careful preparation. Do not attempt this without a Standard Oxygen Tank at minimum. Bring at least 3 Beacons, a full stack of food (Nutrient Blocks recommended), and a backup Oxygen Tank. Mark both the cave entrance and the shrine location with Beacons — getting lost at this depth is fatal.

Food Farm: Setting Up Renewable Resources

Once you have a base with power, you can set up a food farm: plant Creepvine Seed Clusters in exterior growbeds for a renewable Fiber Mesh and food source. Build an interior growbed for Marblemelons or Lantern Fruit — these provide both food and water. Set up a Water Filtration Machine for unlimited clean water. Keep a locker stocked with cured fish and Nutrient Blocks for emergency supplies.

Survival Checklist

  • ✅ Catch Peepers regularly and cook before eating
  • ✅ Process Bladderfish for water at the Fabricator
  • ✅ Craft a Water Bottle as soon as you have Titanium
  • ✅ Never let hunger or thirst drop below 25%
  • ✅ Collect Salt whenever you see it — critical for cooking
  • ✅ Stock Nutrient Blocks for extended expeditions
  • ✅ The Angel Comb is worth the journey — plan carefully
  • ✅ Set up a food farm once you have a base with power

With a solid food and water supply, you can explore freely and stay alive indefinitely. The hunger and thirst system rewards preparation and punishes complacency. Never leave your Lifepod without carrying food and water — the ocean doesn't wait for you to come back.
