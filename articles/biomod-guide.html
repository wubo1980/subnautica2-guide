Subnautica 2 Biomod Guide: Unlock, Best Builds & EA1.1 Preview

Everything about Subnautica 2's DNA modification system. How to find the Bioscanner, build the Bio Lab and Gene Station, every active and passive Biomod ranked, and what the EA1.1 overhaul means for your build.

Biomods are Subnautica 2's most innovative system — DNA modifications that change how your character moves, senses, and survives underwater. Unlike equipment upgrades that go in your inventory, Biomods are biological enhancements applied at the Gene Augmentation Station. With EA1.1 coming soon and promising a major Biomod overhaul, now is the perfect time to master the current system before the changes land.

How to Unlock Biomods

Unlocking the Biomod system requires three steps. You cannot skip ahead — each step is gated by specific materials and exploration progress.

Step 1: Find and Craft the Bioscanner

The Bioscanner is a handheld tool that lets you collect DNA samples from creatures and plants. It's the key that unlocks everything else in the Biomod tree. Unlike the standard Scanner which gives data entries, the Bioscanner extracts genetic material.

Bioscanner Recipe
Location Found as a fragment in the Welcome Center wreckage (Grassy Plateau, 70-100m depth)
Materials 1x Battery + 1x Titanium + 1x Glass + 1x Wiring Kit

To find the Bioscanner fragment, explore the Welcome Center POI in the Grassy Plateau. The wreckage is marked by a distinct orange beacon. Scan the fragment (3 scans needed for full blueprint). Once crafted, equip the Bioscanner in your tool slot alongside your Survival Multitool and Scanner.

How to collect DNA: Swim close to any creature (aggressive or passive) and use the Bioscanner. A DNA sample takes roughly 3 seconds to extract. For plants, hover near the base and scan. Each scan yields one DNA unit. Different species yield different Biomod unlocks.

🧬 First DNA Targets

Prioritize Creepvine (gives Fiber Mesh crafting efficiency), Peepers (unlocks the basic Dash active Biomod), and Bladderfish (unlocks Sea Skimmer passive). These three unlocks give you the strongest early-game advantage. Check our Material Locations guide for where to find each species reliably.

Step 2: Build the Bio Lab

The Bio Lab is a base module where you process raw DNA into usable Biomods. It's a large room that functions like a crafting station specifically for biological modifications.

Bio Lab Construction
Materials 4x Titanium + 2x Glass + 1x Computer Chip + 1x Lubricant
Power Requires 75 power. Ensure your base has sufficient capacity (2+ Solar Panels or equivalent).

Place the Bio Lab as an attached compartment to your existing base. It's best positioned near your Fabricator room for convenience. The Bio Lab has built-in storage for DNA samples and displays your currently researched Biomods.

Step 3: Build the Gene Augmentation Station

This is the station where you actually apply Biomods to your character. It sits inside the Bio Lab and is the final piece of the puzzle.

Gene Augmentation Station
Materials 2x Titanium + 1x Computer Chip + 1x Wiring Kit + 1x Diamond
Usage Interact to open the Biomod menu. Apply Active (1 slot) and Passive (1 slot) modifications.

Diamonds are found in shale outcrops in deep caves below 200m. The best early source is the Jellyshroom Caves — accessible through entrances in the Grassy Plateau. Bring extra oxygen and a Seaglide.

Complete Biomod List

The current EA version has 8 active and 6 passive Biomods. Each costs a specific amount of DNA units. You can equip one active and one passive at a time. Swapping costs no resources — return to the Gene Station and choose a different mod.

Active Biomods (Equip 1)
⚡

Dash

Cost: 3 Peeper DNA | Effect: Short burst of speed (~2 seconds). 15-second cooldown. The most versatile active Biomod — useful for escaping predators, catching fish, and covering ground quickly. Recommended as your first unlock.

🔦

Pathfinder

Cost: 3 Bladderfish DNA | Effect: Leaves a visible trail behind you for 30 seconds. Excellent for cave diving but less useful in open water. Switch to Dash for general exploration.

👁️

Night Vision

Cost: 5 Cave Crawler DNA | Effect: Brightens dark environments for 20 seconds. 45-second cooldown. Useful in deep caves but the cooldown is punishing. Collect Cave Crawler DNA in the Jellyshroom Caves.

🛡️

Sonar Pulse

Cost: 5 Marrowbreach DNA | Effect: Emits a sonar wave revealing nearby creature positions. 30-second cooldown. Situationally valuable in The Depths.

🌊

Deep Pressure

Cost: 5 Cuddlefish DNA | Effect: Reduces oxygen depletion rate by 15% for 60 seconds. 90-second cooldown. Useful for deep cave exploration but rare DNA species required.

Passive Biomods (Equip 1)
💧

Sea Skimmer

Cost: 3 Bladderfish DNA | Effect: Slowly regenerates water over time (~2 units per minute). The single best passive Biomod. Reduces your dependency on Bladderfish and water bottles by roughly 30%.

🍖

Efficient Metabolism

Cost: 3 Peeper DNA | Effect: Food drains 20% slower. Decent for long expeditions but less impactful than Sea Skimmer since food is more abundant than water.

🦈

Predator Scent

Cost: 5 Stalker DNA | Effect: Small predators are less likely to notice you. Reduces aggro range by roughly 25%. Useful for Kelp Forest exploration.

✨

Bioluminescent Glow

Cost: 3 Jellyray DNA | Effect: Emits a soft glow, illuminating a small radius around you. Aesthetic but functionally inferior to the Flashlight tool.

Best Biomod Builds by Playstyle

Solo Explorer

Active: Dash | Passive: Sea Skimmer

The all-rounder build. Dash handles creature evasion and rapid traversal. Sea Skimmer cuts your water dependency in half. This combo works for every biome up to 300m depth. If you only unlock two Biomods, pick these.

Cave Diver

Active: Pathfinder | Passive: Efficient Metabolism

Switch to Pathfinder when navigating the Jellyshroom Caves, The Depths, or any complex cave system. The trail is invaluable for retracing your steps. Efficient Metabolism keeps you fed during extended cave sessions. Recommended: pair with our Oxygen Management guide for maximum dive time.

Co-op Gatherer

Active: Dash | Passive: Sea Skimmer or Predator Scent

In multiplayer, your role as gatherer means covering distance quickly. Dash is essential. Sea Skimmer reduces how often you need to surface for water, keeping your gathering momentum. If gathering in Kelp Forest, swap to Predator Scent to avoid Nibbler swarms.

🧬 Co-op Biomod Coordination

In a 4-player session, assign different Biomod roles: one player takes Aquatic Speed boosts (fast scout), another takes Thermal Resistance (deep zone explorer), a third takes Carrying Capacity (resource gatherer). Coordinate loadouts at the Bio Lab before heading out. See our Co-op Guide for team role strategies.

EA1.1 Preview: What's Changing for Biomods

Unknown Worlds confirmed in the June 4 Dev Vlog that EA1.1 will include a significant Biomod system overhaul. Based on the announcement and IGN's June 5 coverage, here's what we know and what we expect:

Confirmed changes:

  • UI redesign: The current Biomod interface is getting a full overhaul. The Dev Vlog specifically cited "improvements to the Biomod UI" as a priority. Current UI has been criticized for being confusing for new players.
  • More Biomod slots: The current 1 active + 1 passive cap is being expanded. Exact new slot count hasn't been announced, but the roadmap language suggests 2+ active slots and multiple passive slots.
  • New passive modifications: At least 3 new passive Biomods are in development, focused on exploration and survival quality-of-life.

Our predictions based on the Dev Vlog teasers:

  • The Bioscanner DNA economy may be rebalanced — currently some rare species require too many samples for underwhelming unlocks
  • Some current Biomods (Bioluminescent Glow, Sonar Pulse) may receive buffs to make them competitive with top picks like Dash and Sea Skimmer
  • A new "Biomod synergy" system may grant bonus effects when equipping specific active + passive combinations

What this means for you: Don't hoard DNA samples — spend them now to learn the current system. EA1.1 will add more slots and more options, not remove existing unlocks. Any progress you make in the current system carries forward.

🔥 Bottom Line

Unlock the Bioscanner → build the Bio Lab → craft the Gene Station. Start with Dash (active) and Sea Skimmer (passive) — they're the strongest Biomods in the current game. With EA1.1 around the corner bringing more slots and a UI overhaul, the Biomod system will only get better. Master the basics now so you can hit the ground running when the update drops.
